# E-commerce Sales & Customer Analytics | MySQL

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing e-commerce transactional data using MySQL to evaluate sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, regional trends, and order returns.

The objective is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that can support revenue growth, customer retention, and better business decisions.

## Business Problem

How can an e-commerce business understand its revenue drivers, identify valuable customers and high-performing products, evaluate regional performance, and identify areas where product returns may be affecting business performance?

## Project Objectives

- Analyse overall revenue and monthly sales trends.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour and repeat purchase patterns.
- Segment customers based on cumulative spending.
- Compare regional revenue and order performance.
- Analyse return rates across products, categories, and regions.

## Tools & Technologies

- Database: MySQL
- SQL Environment: MySQL Workbench
- SQL Techniques: JOINs, GROUP BY, Aggregate Functions, CASE Statements, Subqueries, Conditional Aggregation, Date Functions and Window Functions.
- Analytical Areas: Revenue Analysis, Customer Segmentation, Product Analysis, Regional Analysis and Return Rate Analysis.

## Key Business Findings

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Gross Revenue | 2,757,346 |
| Net Revenue | 2,049,059 |
| Average Order Value | 3,206.67 |
| Repeat Customer Rate | 91.24% |
| Overall Order Return Rate | 24.9% |
| Highest Revenue Category | Electronics |
| Highest Revenue Region | Asia East |

### Key Insights

- Net revenue in 2024 increased by 47.2% compared with 2023.
- Electronics generated the highest category-level net revenue of approximately 485.6K.
- Smartphone M was the highest revenue-generating product.
- Home recorded the highest category return rate at 27.62%.
- Africa South recorded the highest regional return rate at 31.37%.
- Asia East led regional net revenue with approximately 331.9K.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate return drivers in Home and Accessories categories.
- Monitor product-level revenue alongside return rates and profitability.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-value customers.
- Study the performance drivers behind Asia East's strong revenue contribution.
- Monitor revenue, average order value, repeat purchase rate, and returns regularly.
